# Make your first scene

Three ways in. Two of them need no AI key and no internet.

Get to them all the same way: **long-press Home** → **Admin** → **Scenes** →
**New Scene**.

---

## Before you choose

The fastest good scene is usually **not** built from nothing. Core-First
already has the high-frequency vocabulary and the category structure. If you
want a scene for a specific session, consider:

1. Admin → Scenes → swipe left on **Core-First** → **Duplicate**
2. Rename the copy, trim what you don't need, add what you do

That takes about two minutes and inherits a sane core. The three routes below
are for when you want something genuinely different.

---

## Route 1 — Build from Collections (no AI, no key)

The one to try first. It builds a real multi-page scene out of vocabulary you
already have, instantly, offline.

1. **New Scene** → **No AI — combine packs & classes**
2. Give it a name — "Snack time", "Farm visit"
3. Pick any **Vocabulary Packs** (Farm, Tide Pools, Mealtime, Space, Dinosaurs,
   Vehicles) and any **Word Classes** (actions, feelings, people, food…)
4. **Create**

Each pack and each class you tick becomes its own page, and a home page is
created linking to all of them.

It's blunt — a class page is *every* word of that class, which can be a lot —
but it gets you a working structure in seconds, and trimming is faster than
building. Open the scene, tap a page, and remove what you don't want.

## Route 2 — Describe it, and let the AI build it

Needs an OpenAI key (Admin → Device). Takes 20–40 seconds.

1. **New Scene** → type into **Describe the scene**
2. Be specific about the child and the goal. Compare:
   - ✗ "feelings"
   - ✓ "feelings for a 5-year-old working on frustration vs anger"
3. **Generate Scene**
4. You get a **Scene Preview** — pages, tiles, pictures, navigation, all of it.
   Scroll through the whole thing.
5. **Accept**, **Refine**, or **Cancel**

**Refine** is the underused one. Rather than accepting something that's 80%
right, describe the change — "drop the school pages, add a page about the
dog" — and it revises.

Nothing is created until you tap Accept, and everything is editable afterwards.

**If the scene introduces new words**, the preview says so, and any word the
moderation check flagged is marked. You must resolve those before Accept —
see [Adding vocabulary](adding-vocabulary.md).

## Route 3 — Start from an example

**New Scene** → **Start from an example** → pick a ready-made scene → **Load
Example**. Instant, no key. Good for seeing what a well-formed scene looks like
before building your own. Edit the description first and the button switches to
Generate, using the example as a starting point.

## Route 4 — Manual

Toolbar → **Manual** → name → **Create**. An empty scene and an editor. Use
this when you know exactly what you want and it's small.

---

## After it exists

You land in the scene editor.

- **Scene Info** — name, description, and the **Home Page** picker
- **Focused layout** — trims the scene for 1:1 work: the topical tiles plus a
  short needs strip (hungry/thirsty, help, feelings) and body & health. Off
  gives the full familiar scene. Not available on Core-First.
- **Pages (N)** — every page, with a **HOME** badge on the home page and tile
  counts. Orange and red chips flag pages with words needing review.
- **Add Page** — see [Pages and navigation](pages-and-navigation.md)
- **Eye icon** (toolbar) — preview the scene exactly as the child sees it
- **Sparkle icon** — refine the whole scene with AI
- **Share icon** — export it

**If new words need pictures**, a **New-Word Art** section appears at the top
with a count and a Generate button. Art generation runs in the background and
survives leaving the editor. **Generate all styles** makes art for every tile
style rather than just the active one — slower, but the scene then looks right
if you or a family switch styles later.

---

## Making the child actually see it

Creating a scene doesn't activate it. **Admin → Scenes → swipe right → Activate.**

Then check your work the way the child will meet it: leave Admin, and use the
scene. Preview is good; the real grid on the real device is better.
