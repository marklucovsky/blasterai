# The Me page

Every profile has a page of its own: the **Me page**, opened by the heart on the rail. It sits on top of whatever board is current, so it travels with the person rather than with the board. Aubrey's Me page holds Aubrey's people, places and favorite things whichever board she is using today.

The Me page is more than one fixed page. It can change with where the user came from, take in pages other people keep up to date, and open topic boards without changing the main board. This guide covers all of that, and how a speech-language pathologist can build it once and send it to every family.

Everything here is in **Settings › Profile › Me Page** for the current profile, or **All Profiles** › a profile › **Me Page** for anyone else.

![Settings › Profile › Me Page, with one Always item, three Pinned tiles, a Race Car topic, and two items for the I page](/img/guides/me-settings.jpg)

## The page itself

**Edit Aubrey's Page** opens the Me page in the page editor, the same one every board uses. What you put there stays exactly where you put it. Nothing below ever covers it.

The first cell under the navigation bar is always **My Topics**. The editor shows it dimmed, with a lock: it can't be moved or covered, so it is in the same place on every Me page and every device.

New profiles start with an almost empty Me page: My Topics and nothing else. That is deliberate. Most of what a Me page shows usually comes from the rules below, which you can share, rather than from tiles placed by hand on one device.

## Rules: what gets added

Three kinds of rule add to the page each time it opens. None of them covers anything on the page itself.

- **Always** adds its words and folders every time the Me page opens.
- **Pinned** puts tiles in cells you choose. If the page already uses a cell, the pinned tile moves to the next free one. The Pinned editor shows My Topics locked in its cell too.
- **Me Page by Page** adds words and folders only when the user presses the heart *from* a particular page. Pressing it from Food can bring up favorite snacks; pressing it from Places, the park and grandma's house. A page counts by its name, on BlasterAI 60 or any of your boards.

An Always list or a page's list holds **items**. An item is a page to take words from (a BlasterAI 60 page or a topic's page) or words you pick yourself (**Words…**). For a page you choose how many of its words to bring in (none, 1 to 8, or all) and whether to add a **Folder** to it as well, so what doesn't fit is one tap away. Words already on the Me page are left out rather than shown twice.

### Where added tiles land

The layout is fixed so that the page stays learnable:

- **Words** go below your own tiles, in list order, after an empty row when there is room. They use columns 1 to 7.
- **Folders** go in column 8, then into the free cells of columns 9 and 10.
- What doesn't fit is left off. Turn on the folder for that item to keep the rest one tap away.

Put what should always stay in one place on the page itself or under Pinned. What changes from page to page goes in rules and fills the space you leave.

## Preview

**Preview** shows the Me page as the user will see it, from Home or **Coming from** any page that has rules. Every added tile is marked by where it came from:

- a pin: Pinned, or on an included page;
- an orange arrow: moved, because its cell was taken;
- a dashed blue edge: Always;
- a dashed orange edge: for the page the user came from.

**Show Zones** shades where words flow (green), where folders go first (purple), and the columns that are filled only when the rest is full (gray). Tap any tile to see where it comes from.

## Includes: Me pages that other people keep up to date

A Me page can **include** other Me pages. Their words, folders and pinned tiles are added after this page's own, without covering them.

This is how a class works. Ms. K keeps a Me page called *Room 4* with the class's people, the schedule words and the classroom jobs. Each student's Me page includes Room 4. When Ms. K changes Room 4 and sends it again, every student who has it gets the change; nobody's own page is touched.

**Include a Me Page** lists the Me pages on this device and the ones you have received. An included page that isn't on this device yet shows as *(not on this device)* until it arrives.

## My Topics

**My Topics** holds boards the user opens from the Me page without changing the main board: a Thanksgiving dinner board, a zoo trip, a Sunday School board. Tap a topic to also show it for particular pages, so pressing the heart from Food brings the Thanksgiving board forward.

Add a board to My Topics with the heart in the board editor's toolbar, or **Add a Topic** here.

Until it has a topic, the My Topics folder is dimmed. In Talk mode it does nothing; in Setup mode, tapping it explains what it is for and how to add one.

## Sharing a Me page

**Share Aubrey's Me Page** sends one board file holding the Me page, the pages it includes and its topic boards. Whoever opens it can add it to the Me page of a profile on their device. Sending it again updates it there in place. They won't be asked again, and their own additions stay.

For a speech-language pathologist with several students, the usual setup is:

1. Make a profile on your device for each student, as a stand-in. Their Me pages can include your class pages.
2. Build each Me page there, checking it in Preview.
3. Share it to the family. When you change it, share it again.

Your own Me page, the one on your profile, can be shared the same way. It arrives under your Author name (**Settings › Advanced › Authoring**), so two teachers' pages never collide.

## What it doesn't do yet

- Rules are by page name, not by board. "Food" on any board triggers the Food list.
- There is no way to schedule a rule (mornings only, school days only).
